⑦ METHODOLOGY

Fund Flow
The primary capital-flow metric comes directly from the remote etf_flows.csv dataset. The dashboard does not reverse-engineer Fund Flow from Shares Outstanding × Price.

1D / 7D / 30D
1D is the latest available Fund Flow. 7D and 30D are the sum of the daily Fund Flow observations inside the corresponding calendar window.

Shares Outstanding
Shares are loaded independently from etf_shares.csv. Shares Δ is displayed as an additional indicator and is not treated as Fund Flow.

AUM
AUM is loaded from etf_aum.csv. If AUM is unavailable for an ETF, the dashboard falls back to:

Shares Outstanding × Market Price

Flow / AUM
Fund Flow divided by AUM provides a relative measure of capital movement across ETFs and categories of different sizes.
