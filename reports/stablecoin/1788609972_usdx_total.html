INDICATOR GUIDE

CURRENT SUPPLY

The current circulating supply of USDT and USDC. It represents the total tracked stablecoin liquidity.

NET SUPPLY FLOW

Current supply minus previous supply.

Positive (+): supply increased.
Negative (-): supply decreased.

1D / 1W / 30D

Net supply change compared with 1 day, 1 week and 30 days ago.
Numbers in parentheses are percentage change of circulating supply.

30D AVG DAILY FLOW

Average daily net supply change during the last 30 days.

30D FLOW VOLATILITY

Standard deviation of daily net supply flows during the last 30 days.

ANOMALY SCORE (σ)

Z = (Today's Flow − 30D Average Flow) ÷ 30D Flow Standard Deviation

SIGNAL

Z ≥ +2 → HIGH INFLOW 🔥
+1 ~ +2 → MODERATE INFLOW 🟢
−1 ~ +1 → NORMAL ⚪
−2 ~ −1 → MODERATE OUTFLOW 🟠
Z ≤ −2 → HIGH OUTFLOW 🔴

DATA NOTE

Net Supply Flow measures the net change in circulating supply.

It does not necessarily represent the gross amount of stablecoins issued or redeemed.
